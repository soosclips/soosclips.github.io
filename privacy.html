Privacy Policy

Effective 18 November 2026

This policy covers the Soos Clips website and the Soos Clips Pipeline application (the "App"). The App is an internal tool operated by Soos Clips to upload, schedule and publish videos to social media accounts that Soos Clips owns, using the official APIs of YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and X.

What the App accesses

The App connects only to accounts owned by Soos Clips. Through platform authorization (OAuth) it receives access tokens that let it publish videos, read basic profile information, and read view and engagement statistics for those accounts. It does not request, access or store data about any other person.

What we store

Access tokens and basic account identifiers are stored locally on computers controlled by Soos Clips. Video files and their titles, captions and performance statistics are also stored locally. We do not sell, share or transfer this information to anyone. We do not use advertising trackers or analytics on this website.

Third parties

Publishing uses the APIs of Google/YouTube, TikTok, Meta and X. Their handling of data is governed by their own policies. Google API data is used in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time from the connected-apps settings of each platform, which invalidates the stored tokens. To request deletion of anything else, email soos.clip@gmail.com.

Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page. Questions: soos.clip@gmail.com.
